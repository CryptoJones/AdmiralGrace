# Engine options

Every judgment call the engine makes is an option, set per run with `--option NAME VALUE` on `ag image <bin> pseudocode` and `ag image <bin> export`, and on `ag-ui`. This file is generated by `ag catalog --markdown` from the registry in `crates/ag-engine/src/options.rs`; `ag catalog --check` and `cargo test` fail when it is stale. Edit the registry, not this file.

## Symptom index

If the output shows… | Try…
--- | ---
`not lifted yet: rcx is read before it is written` on an ELF built for the Microsoft convention | `--option abi win64`
`not lifted yet: rdi is read before it is written` on a PE built with a System V compiler | `--option abi sysv`
a function you know takes more arguments than its signature shows, because the tail of them is only passed on | `--option parameters convention`
`long a` in a signature where nothing reads `a` | `--option parameters last-read`
`eax`, `eax_2` and `edi` declared as locals, reading like a listing and not like C | `--option merged-register-names numbered`
`var_1` and `var_2` where you want to see which register the compiler used | `--option merged-register-names register`
`local_14` next to a listing whose offsets are decimal | `--option stack-slot-names decimal`
`local_20` next to a listing that says `[rbp-0x14]` | `--option stack-slot-names hex`
`counter` where you want the address the instruction named, to check it against the bytes | `--option pointer-loads cast`
`*(int *)0x4010` where the image has a name for the data | `--option pointer-loads named`
`b + a` and `a + b` in two functions that do the same thing | `--option operand-order canonical`
`a + b` where the listing says `add esi, edi` and you want the C to follow it | `--option operand-order instruction`
`(unsigned int)a < (unsigned int)b` cluttering every bounds check | `--option unsigned-compare bare`
`a < b` where you need to know whether a negative `a` passes | `--option unsigned-compare cast`
a `cmp` in the listing that the C says nothing about | `--option dead-compares keep`
`__cmp(a, b);` statements that do nothing | `--option dead-compares drop`
`return printf(a);` where the listing ends in `jmp`, and you want the functions that jump rather than call to stand out as refusals | `--option tail-calls refuse`
`not lifted yet: a tail call to printf at 0x…` on a function that ends in `jmp printf` | `--option tail-calls return`
a `for` whose step is not the increment you expected, or whose init is a statement that belongs before the loop | `--option loop-form while`
`i = 0; while (i < n) { …; i = i + 1; }` where a `for` would read better | `--option loop-form for`
`goto label_…;` to a `return` a few lines up | `--option shared-tail duplicate`
the same `return` statements repeated in two arms | `--option shared-tail goto`
`0x64` where the source said `100` | `--option constant-style decimal`
`8` where the source said `0x8`, a mask or an offset | `--option constant-style hex`
`16777216` where `0x1000000` would be read at a glance | `--option constant-style mixed`
bodies indented two spaces next to source indented four | `--option indent 4`

## Lift

Option | Tier | Takes | Default | What it decides
--- | --- | --- | --- | ---
`abi` | analysis | `auto` \| `sysv` \| `win64` | `auto` | The integer calling convention: read from the image's language, or System V (`rdi, rsi, rdx, rcx, r8, r9`) or Microsoft x64 (`rcx, rdx, r8, r9`) whatever the image says.
`parameters` | analysis | `last-read` \| `convention` | `last-read` | Which argument registers are parameters: the ones through the last one read, a gap declared `long`, or every register of the convention.
`merged-register-names` | rendering | `register` \| `numbered` | `register` | How a register that differs where two paths join is named once it is a variable: after the register (`eax`, `eax_2`), or by number (`var_1`, `var_2`).
`stack-slot-names` | rendering | `hex` \| `decimal` | `hex` | How a stack slot is named, by its offset from the entry stack pointer: `local_14` in hex, or `local_20` in decimal.
`pointer-loads` | rendering | `named` \| `cast` | `named` | A load or store at an address the image names as data: the data's name (`counter`), or the typed cast every other address gets (`*(int *)0x4010`).
`operand-order` | rendering | `instruction` \| `canonical` | `instruction` | The operands of `+`, `*`, `&`, `|` and `^`: in the order the instruction names them (`b + a`), or canonical, variables alphabetical and constants last (`a + b`).
`unsigned-compare` | rendering | `cast` \| `bare` | `cast` | A comparison whose condition code is unsigned (`jb`, `ja`, `jbe`, `jae`): both sides cast, `(unsigned int)a < (unsigned int)b`, or written bare, `a < b`.
`dead-compares` | transform | `drop` \| `keep` | `drop` | A `cmp` or `test` whose flags no branch reads: dropped, or kept as the statement `__cmp(a, b);` or `__test(a, b);`.
`tail-calls` | transform | `return` \| `refuse` | `return` | A `jmp` to another function with the stack as it was at entry: lifted as the call and the return it stands for, `return callee(args);` — a function that is only the jump is a thunk, printed as `// thunk for callee` — or refused, naming the callee.

## Structure

Option | Tier | Takes | Default | What it decides
--- | --- | --- | --- | ---
`loop-form` | transform | `for` \| `while` | `for` | A counted loop, `init; while (cond) { …; step }`: folded into `for (init; cond; step)`, or left as the `while` with its init before and its step last.
`shared-tail` | transform | `goto` \| `duplicate` | `goto` | A run of statements two paths share that neither branch dominates: reached by `goto` to a label on it, or copied into the second path when it runs straight to a `return`.

## Emit

Option | Tier | Takes | Default | What it decides
--- | --- | --- | --- | ---
`constant-style` | rendering | `mixed` \| `decimal` \| `hex` | `mixed` | How a constant is spelled: decimal below 10 and hex from 10 up, all decimal, or all hex.
`indent` | rendering | an integer, 1 to 8 | `2` | The spaces one level of a body is indented by.

## Tiers

A *transform* changes what statements there are; an *analysis* changes what the engine concludes about the code; a *rendering* changes how the same conclusion is spelled.
